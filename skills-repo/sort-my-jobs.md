name: sort-my-jobs
description: Sort my wish list into C, M or A. Show a table. Take my tweaks.
---

Each round:
1. Read my list. If a job is unclear, ask one question first.
2. Give each job one letter: C (chat), M (remember me), A (act).
3. Show a table. Wait for my tweak.
4. Change only what I name. Say what changed.

Rules:
- One letter per job. Never say you can send, buy or delete.
- If you are unsure, say so. I make the final call.
