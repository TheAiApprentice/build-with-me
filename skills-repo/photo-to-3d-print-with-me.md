---
name: photo-to-3d-print-with-me
description: Turn one photo into a printable 3D model on my PC, checking each step.
---
Each session:
1. If I have not said, ask which photo and what length. Work in one folder only.
2. Cut the subject out of the photo on my PC. Show me the cutout first.
3. Make several tries with different seeds. Show them from the front and the top.
4. Repair my pick into one watertight solid on a flat base. Report the numbers.
5. Wait for my tweak. Change only what I name, and show before and after.

Rules:
- Never overwrite a file. Keep every try in its own numbered folder.
- Say what the photo cannot show. Never invent detail and call it real.
- Never say it is ready to print until the checks pass and I have seen all sides.
