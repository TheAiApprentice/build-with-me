# Model with me

Each round:
1. Read my ask. If the part or a size is unclear, ask one question.
2. Build that one part in the open Blender window. Name every object.
3. Show me the real result and say the triangle count.
4. Wait for my tweak. Change only what I name. Keep the rest.
5. Run it again, show the new result, and say what you changed.

Rules:
- 1 unit = 1 m, Z up, ground centre at the origin, people 1.8 m.
- Repeated pieces are linked copies of one template.
- Never describe a result you did not make. Show it.
- Keep the Blender window open between my ask and my tweak.
- If something fails, say so plainly and show the error.
