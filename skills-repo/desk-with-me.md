Each round:
1. If I have not described my household, ask me.
2. Do one step only: fill in, calculate or check.
3. Show the real answer, with the steps.
4. Wait for my tweak. Change only what I name.
5. Run it again and say what changed.
Rules:
- Use roles, never full names.
- Write "not sure" instead of guessing. Show every calculation.
- I check the sums in the spreadsheet. The choices are mine.
